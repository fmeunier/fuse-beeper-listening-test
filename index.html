Introduction

This experiment compares ZX Spectrum beeper emulation with recordings of real ZX Spectrum hardware.

For each trial you will hear a Reference recording from a real ZX Spectrum, plus emulator renderings A and B. Listen to the reference, then A and B, and select whichever emulator sample sounds closer to the reference. If neither sounds meaningfully closer, choose No meaningful preference.

There are 18 trials. The test should take approximately 10–15 minutes.

Listening instructions

  • Use headphones or reasonable speakers in a reasonably quiet environment.
  • Choose a comfortable listening level before beginning and avoid changing it during the test.
  • Replay Reference, A, and B as often as needed.
  • Judge similarity to the reference, not which sample sounds subjectively “nicer”.
  • Use “No meaningful preference” rather than guessing when a difference is not meaningful.
